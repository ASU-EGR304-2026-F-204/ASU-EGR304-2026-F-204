## Team Charter

Our team is focused on designing and creating a wearable heart rate monitor that provides accurate, real-time heart rate data and clear value to its target users. We will follow a complete engineering workflow—from defining performance requirements and selecting sensors to designing the circuitry, enclosure, and user interface—to produce a reliable, manufacturable prototype. The device will be evaluated for measurement accuracy, comfort, durability, battery life, and ease of use. Through strong collaboration, testing, and professional documentation, we aim to develop a high-quality heart rate monitor that reflects real-world engineering standards and is ready for further development, user testing, or market evaluation.

## Product Mission Statement

Our mission is to design and develop a reliable, user-friendly wearable heart rate monitor that delivers accurate, real-time health data to help users better understand and manage their physical activity and cardiovascular wellness. By applying a disciplined engineering process, rigorous testing, and collaborative design practices, we will create a comfortable, durable, and manufacturable prototype that meets defined performance standards and has a clear path toward further development and market evaluation.
